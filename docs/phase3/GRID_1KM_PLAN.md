# Plan: the first 1 km² area (16 blocks)

Status: grid and per-block configs prepared 2026-10-30; nothing trained. Builds on the 19-view recipe (1.36 m
registered lidar MAE on the central block) and the split experiment (seams invisible; joining cost 0.02–0.04 m).

## Grid

`configs/jax068-grid-1km.json`, generated by `scripts/make-grid-1km.py`: 4 × 4 cells of 250 m in the JAX_068 anchor
ENU frame, half-open shared-grid ownership as in the split experiment. Rows A–D run north to south, columns 1–4 west to
east.

| Row | north (m) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 0 … 250 | A1 | A2 | A3 | A4 |
| B | −250 … 0 | B1 | B2 | B3 | B4 |
| C | −500 … −250 | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 |
| D | −750 … −500 | D1 | D2 | D3 | D4 |

Columns span east −500 … 500 m. The grid sits one row south of centred: the 2015-12-21 acquisition
(`1040010016BADB00`) does not reach north of about +250 m (it missed the northern row of a centred grid by up to
614 px). With this grid, all 20 acquisitions cover every cell plus its 20 m halo at heights −50 … 300 m; the smallest
margin is 70 px (that acquisition), and every other acquisition clears it by at least 4,000 px.

Each cell has `configs/grid-1km/<cell>-block.json` (pilot crop size, halo and height bracket) and
`configs/grid-1km/<cell>-19v.json`. These are identical to the 19-view approved selection apart from the block path:
the same 19 training acquisitions and the May 2 holdout.

## Accuracy labelling

Lidar is not an input, only a check. The DFC2019 lidar covers the meeting point of A2, A3, B2 and B3, so those four
cells (each partly) and their shared seams can be measured, using the corrected comparison from the split experiment:
each cell against the single-block model on the same area, and seams by the step beyond the lidar's own step. The other
12 cells have no independent reference (3DEP did not qualify) and are labelled "built with the measured 19-view method;
unmeasured" in their releases.

## Viewer

Within 1 km, blocks currently refine completely to LOD0 with a 192 MB cache each. With 16 adjacent blocks that could
load most of the area at full detail at once. Before publishing the grid, limit full detail to the nearest few blocks and
measure GPU memory with the 16 releases.

## After training

1. **Lidar cells and seams.** Rules and same-area baselines are pre-registered in `configs/grid-1km-decision.json`
   (training/grid-1km-baseline/). Measure with `pipeline.grid_seams`, which reuses the split experiment's seam
   measurement and rotates horizontal seams into its vertical frame:

   ```bash
   .venv-imagery/bin/python -m pipeline.grid_seams --grid configs/jax068-grid-1km.json \
     --decision configs/grid-1km-decision.json --truth artifacts/reference/dfc2019-jax068 \
     --inputs <inputs.json: {cell: {export, depth, validation}} for A2, A3, B2, B3> \
     --output artifacts/validation/grid-1km-seams.json
   ```

2. **Review catalogue.** Publish all 16 into a separate catalogue first and view them together:
   `pipeline.job <job> --from publish --publish-to artifacts/releases-grid-review --activate`.
3. **Live.** After review, publish into `artifacts/releases` with `--activate`. Cells failing a rule are published
   inactive and discussed first. Retire the central single block (`jax068-2015`) only once the grid is live.
